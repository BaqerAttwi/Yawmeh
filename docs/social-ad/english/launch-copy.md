# Your day needs a sidekick

28-second English social ad. Vertical 1080 × 1920. Existing Sage, Nova, and Pip character art is animated using the site's four-frame greeting sprites. Product footage uses browser-local demo records.

## Creative sequence

- 0–3s: “Too much on your mind?” — hook.
- 3–6.5s: Sage reveal — your everyday companion.
- 6.5–10.5s: Tasks — make room for what matters.
- 10.5–14.5s: Money — know where your money goes.
- 14.5–19s: Companion chat — ask it, plan it, do it.
- 19–23s: Sage, Nova, and Pip — your kind of energy.
- 23–28s: Brand and closing invitation.

## Audio and source notes

Music is an original, deterministic electronic composition generated for this ad. Voiceover is synthesized locally with Microsoft Zira Desktop. No third-party music recording is used. This note does not establish ownership or third-party rights for the user's existing character artwork.

Voice lines: “Your day needs a sidekick. Meet Yawmeh.” and “Your everyday. A little clearer.”

This is a local marketing asset. No social account is accessed and nothing is published. The ad makes no assertion that public hosting, signups, or a particular launch date are available.

Scripts use the runtime and browser paths available on this PC. Run `capture.mjs` while the local app preview is running, then `narrate.ps1`, `render.mjs`, and `finish.py`. `ad.html` is the editable animated composition.
